What each button does

The desktop map uses the touchpad as the mouse. Change any button here. Keyboard choices wait for the next key you press.

Pointer

The DualShock 4 touchpad is read from the pad itself. Xbox and other pads do not have that touchpad, so turn on the left stick if you want them to move the pointer. Pressing the touchpad is a normal button in the map.
